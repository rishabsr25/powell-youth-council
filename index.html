Our Mission

We believe anything can be changed. Our mission is to give more voice and power to the youth of Powell — and our vision is to preserve and improve the beauty of this city for the people who'll inherit it.

Who We Are

Students of Powell, for Powell.

Our members range from incoming high school freshmen to first-year college students — nearly all of us living in or near the City of Powell. We're a platform for students to bring attention to the issues that matter here.

What We Are

A governing body, run by students.

The Powell Youth Council functions as a student governing body for the city — a structured way for young people to work directly on the issues facing our community, not just talk about them.

Agenda Item No. 01

What we do, in practice.

Three standing priorities guide every project the Council takes on.

01 / Advocate

Push for developments Powell needs

We raise issues that affect Powell's youth directly with the people who can act on them, and push for the developments our community needs.

02 / Organize

Run events across the city

From community gatherings to student-led initiatives, we plan and run events that bring Powell's youth together around shared causes.

03 / Volunteer

Show up for local organizations

We volunteer directly with organizations across Powell, putting in the hours that back our advocacy up with action.

How to join the Council.

No prior experience required — just a willingness to show up for Powell.

Reach out

Send us a message through the contact form below and tell us a little about yourself — what grade you're in, and what you'd want to work on.

Sit in on a meeting

Come see how the Council operates firsthand before committing. It's the easiest way to know if it's the right fit.

Get sworn in

Once you're ready, you'll officially join as a member and get plugged into an active project or committee.
